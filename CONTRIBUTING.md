# Contributing to Repalis

Thank you for your interest in Repalis!

## 🚫 We're not accepting outside contributions

Repalis is open source, but **we are not accepting contributions from outside collaborators at this time**.

This applies to all changes, including bug fixes, features, documentation and typo fixes.

## How you can still help

- **Questions, bugs and feature requests**: reach us from the [Repalis dashboard](https://repalis.com/dashboard).
- **Security vulnerabilities**: follow our [security policy](./SECURITY.md) and report privately through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/repaliscom/Repalis/security/advisories/new).
- **Forks**: Repalis is licensed under the [Apache License, Version 2.0](./LICENSE). You're free to fork the project and adapt it under the terms of that license.

## For the Repalis team

Set up your environment with [`DEVELOPMENT.md`](./DEVELOPMENT.md), and follow the conventions in [`AGENTS.md`](./AGENTS.md) and the per-area guides it links to.

We'll update this document if our contribution policy changes.
