## What is Repalis?

**Turn codebases, software architectures, and intelligent workloads into sustainable revenue.**

**Repalis** (formerly KodeDock) is an open-source, developer-first platform that unites a **verified digital marketplace** with a **complete monetization and billing infrastructure**. 

It solves the two biggest challenges software creators and builders face:
1. **For Creators & Sellers:** A place to distribute and monetize production-ready codebases, full-stack boilerplates, microservice architectures, and AI tools with automated cryptographic licensing and global payments.
2. **For Builders & Buyers:** A curated catalog of verified, production-grade architectural templates with instant delivery, versioned updates, and one-click access to private repositories and developer perks.

Instead of spending months stitching together separate payment gateways, licensing logic, tax compliance, file storage, and buyer portals, Repalis delivers the entire commerce lifecycle out of the box.

## Core Capabilities: What Repalis Does

### 1. The Codebase & Software Architecture Marketplace
Sell and distribute production software assets with enterprise-grade reliability:
- **Verified Boilerplates & Starter Kits:** List, showcase, and monetize modern web apps, mobile boilerplates, backend microservices, and AI starter templates.
- **Versioned Releases & Checksums:** Support multiple releases per product with automated SHA-256 integrity checksums and changelogs.
- **Secure Expiring Download Links:** Automated, signed, rate-limited download URLs so code packages cannot be shared publicly or pirated.
- **Live Demo & Tech Stack Tagging:** Showcase live previews, documentation links, and standardized framework tags (e.g., Next.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Tailwind).

### 2. Cryptographic Software Licensing & Asset Protection
Protect your software and automate digital entitlements:
- **Cryptographic License Key Generation:** Automatically issue unique, offline-verifiable license keys upon purchase for desktop, CLI, or native applications.
- **Activation & Device Limits:** Track, validate, activate, and deactivate license keys via lightweight REST API endpoints.
- **Private GitHub Repository Access:** Automatically grant paying customers or sponsors immediate invite access to private GitHub repositories and revoke upon refund/cancellation.
- **Discord Community Role Sync:** Automatically grant community roles tied to verified purchases or active subscriber tiers.

### 3. Universal Monetization & Billing Engine
Ship any monetization model your software demands:
- **One-Time Purchases:** Sell lifetime licenses, digital downloads, datasets, and standalone codebases.
- **Recurring Subscriptions:** Support flexible monthly, annual, or custom billing intervals with automated proration, dunning, and card updates.
- **Seat-Based & Team Licenses:** Charge organizational teams per seat with invitation flows and member management.
- **AI Token & Usage-Based Metering:** Meter tokens, LLM API calls, autonomous agent executions, GPU seconds, and storage with event-level granularity.
- **Credits & Prepaid Wallets:** Issue, deduct, top-up, and expire developer credits seamlessly.
- **Coupons & Custom Discounts:** Create percentage discounts, fixed-amount vouchers, and time-limited promotional campaigns.

### 4. Global Hosted Checkout & Automated Tax Compliance
- **Frictionless Hosted Checkouts:** Branded, mobile-ready checkout flows supporting credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and localized payment methods.
- **Merchant of Record (MoR):** Worldwide sales tax, EU VAT, and digital services tax calculated and handled automatically.
- **Compliant Invoicing:** Automatically generate and email PDF receipts and tax-compliant business invoices for every transaction.

### 5. Dual Portals: Creator Studio & Buyer Developer Portal
- **Creator Studio (Seller Dashboard):** Manage product catalogs, upload versions, review customer purchases, inspect analytics, and track automatic payouts.
- **Buyer Developer Portal:** A dedicated, self-service dashboard where buyers can download their purchased codebases, manage active license keys, review invoices, and manage subscriptions.
- **Platform Backoffice (Admin Panel):** An internal operations panel for platform owners to inspect transactions, monitor AUP/KYC reviews, oversee creator payouts, and inspect system tasks.

## SDKs & Developer Experience

Repalis is engineered developer-first with clean abstractions, complete OpenAPI contracts, and type-safe libraries:

- **JavaScript & TypeScript SDK:** Seamless integrations for Node.js, Next.js, Nuxt, Astro, Svelte, and modern client applications.
- **Python SDK:** Built with modern Python 3.14 standards for FastAPI, Django, Flask, and background worker pipelines.
- **Webhook Engine:** Real-time event notifications with cryptographic signature verification for orders, subscriptions, license activations, and payout events.
- **OpenAPI 3.1 Specifications:** Standardized specifications allowing developers to generate custom client SDKs in any programming language.

## Transparent Creator Economics

Repalis is built on transparent, creator-friendly fee structures:
- **Open Source:** 100% free to inspect, customize, and self-host under the Apache 2.0 license.
- **Platform Marketplace:** Transparent transaction fee model with creator payouts and automated tax deductions.
- **Startup Program:** Eligible early-stage builders and open-source creators can apply for preferential fee tiers and growth support.
